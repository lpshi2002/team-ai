Superseded by `DEC-0003`. This record preserved the richer deliberation-memory design, but the final choice is the lighter team-member harness with only the safety fields needed to prevent false memory.

## Question

Should the harness be framed as a minimal v0 that adds memory, graph, and retrieval features later?

## Decision

No. The final architecture should include source-grounded evidence, deliberation events, temporal validity, conditional team priors, independent judgment, team-position reconstruction, critique, and synthesis from the start.

## Rationale

- The user's target is not a quick MVP. It is the most accurate team-like harness design regardless of implementation cost.
- Team memory must trace back to source PDF spans, pages, and meeting events.
- Meeting content must distinguish external knowledge, team interpretation, individual opinion, explicit team position, and later correction.
- The AI's independent assessment must be generated separately from the reconstructed team view so it does not merely imitate the team history.

## Strongest Countercase

The structure is heavier than the current corpus requires and may feel like scaffolding before ingestion code exists.

## Why The Decision Holds

The extra structure is not product polish. It prevents false team memory: treating studied material as preference, one person's opinion as consensus, or a past decision as permanently binding.
